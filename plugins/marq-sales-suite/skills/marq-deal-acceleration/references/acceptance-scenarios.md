# Acceptance Scenarios

These are synthetic behavioral fixtures, not real Marq customer records. They test judgment without storing customer-identifiable CRM data in the skill. Before production approval, run the same checks against five to ten user-approved historical deals whose outcomes are known.

## 1. Genuinely healthy deal

Recorded in Procurement. In the last seven business days the champion introduced procurement and security, security intake was submitted, redlines have an owner/date, the economic buyer confirmed the critical event and investment, and the mutual plan fits the close date.

Expected: actual Procurement, Green, Commit may be supported, continue the current plan with at most one precise next step. Do not add meetings.

## 2. False momentum

Recorded in Closing with ten seller activities in two weeks. Customer last gave a substantive response 35 days ago; proposal and contract were sent unsolicited; next step is “follow up Friday”; close date has moved twice.

Expected: Red, actual stage no later than Opportunity Strategy/Solution Confirmation based on earlier evidence, unsupported close date, Not forecasted, direct status validation plus forecast/date correction.

## 3. Stalled but recoverable

Recorded in Opportunity Strategy. Customer validated pain and fit, then paused for a reorganization. Champion clearly named a new VP's October planning cycle as the restart trigger and agreed to reconnect on October 15.

Expected: Yellow or Red active momentum, move to nurture rather than generic chasing or disqualification, preserve the dated re-entry trigger, remove from current forecast.

## 4. Single-threaded deal

Recorded in Group Consensus. One director is responsive and enthusiastic but has made no introductions. Economic buyer, IT, users, and procurement have never engaged; director says “everyone is on board.”

Expected: actual stage Opportunity Strategy, Yellow, multithread to the specific missing decision roles, block Commit.

## 5. No quantified impact

Recorded in Solution Confirmation. Stakeholders agree the workflow is frustrating and Marq fits, but no one can explain cost, risk, strategic consequence, or priority relative to other projects.

Expected: actual stage no later than Opportunity Strategy, Yellow, business-case/discovery action with customer-owned inputs; no invented ROI.

## 6. Late stage without economic-buyer access

Recorded in Procurement. Security intake is active and technical fit is confirmed, but budget authority is unnamed and the champion refuses access. Procurement started only as an exploratory vendor check.

Expected: not actual Procurement for forecast purposes; Yellow at best, no Commit, multithread to the budget/decision authority or directly test the authority path.

## 7. Should be disqualified

Customer says the issue is not a priority, no budget will be requested, required functionality is unavailable, and they are only collecting market information. No future trigger exists.

Expected: Red, disqualify/close lost with evidence. Do not recommend a pilot, executive call, nurture without a trigger, or more content.

## 8. Recovered from risk

The deal was Red after a champion departure. A new problem owner revalidated impact, introduced the economic buyer and security lead, completed two dated commitments, and rebuilt a feasible plan around a customer-owned event.

Expected: Green only after the new evidence; cite the recovery sequence, not historical seller activity. Continue the plan and retain a secondary stakeholder-risk note.

## Evaluation checklist

For each scenario verify that the output:

- distinguishes recorded from actual stage;
- distinguishes customer progress from seller activity;
- cites dated evidence for stage, momentum, blocker, close date, and forecast;
- identifies one primary constraint rather than a symptom list;
- recommends no more than two actions;
- blocks unsupported Commit/close dates;
- recommends nurture, forecast removal, or disqualification when warranted;
- never invents urgency, ROI, buyer commitments, or quotes;
- proposes CRM corrections but performs no writes.
