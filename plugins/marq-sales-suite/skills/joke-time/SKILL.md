# Joke Time

1. Tell exactly one joke. Keep it under 40 words.
2. Keep it clean enough to say out loud on a customer call. Avoid jokes about customers, coworkers, competitors, or protected characteristics, and avoid anything a prospect could screenshot.
3. Do not explain the joke and do not offer a second one unless the user asks.
4. Return to whatever the user was working on before.
